# Duplicate-content safety boundary

Correlation, identical transcripts, and identical speakers nominate comparisons. They do not prove
that one whole clip is redundant: two cuts can share most of a recording and still contain different
words at their boundaries.

New automatic exclusions require complete decoded clip equivalence, allowing only a constant DC
offset. The Python candidate checker returns **unknown**, not clean or excludable, for correlated
non-identical content. The Rust manifest writer independently verifies the current source hashes
and the complete decoded 16-kHz clip waveforms before appending new exclusions. This also protects
against a manifest generated by an older, overly permissive checker. No guessed acoustic threshold
or source-cut change is introduced. The deliberate cost is lower automatic coverage: uncertain
re-encodes, differing boundaries and partial overlaps require review rather than deletion.

Existing immutable manifests and exact retries remain readable. Historical decisions and credits
must not be erased, transferred between transcripts, or retroactively repriced. A reviewer's exposure
follows every applied duplicate-family link, including retired canonical chains. **Opinions do not**:
agreement and final text remain bound to the original reviewed revision. Queue selection and both
attributed first-opinion and independent-opinion transactions recheck family exposure. Effective
history means undo remains meaningful. Anonymous owner adjudication is not a named paid reviewer.

## Required regressions

- Shared prefix with different ending: unresolved, never automatically excluded.
- Shifted or longer cut: keep the correlation evidence, require content review.
- Mixed sample rates: preserve source-clock comparison; uncertain content stays unresolved.
- New manifest with persuasive correlation metadata but non-equivalent actual clips: atomic refusal.
- Retired twin, including multi-hop canonical retirement: do not serve or pay its reviewer again.
- Another eligible reviewer remains able to provide an independent opinion.
- Existing opinions, credits, exact operation retries, undo and immutable migrations remain intact.

The audio regressions live in `test_dataset_duplicates.py` and `test_check_dataset_duplicates.py`;
authority regressions are in the Rust `review_pool` tests. `test_consensus_review_canon.py` pins all
three family guard boundaries and both manifest writer checks in the policy sweep. Passing these
tests is not full browser/playback field acceptance or final dataset certification. Human resolution,
voice identity, overlap qualification, and safe dataset splits still need their own evidence.
